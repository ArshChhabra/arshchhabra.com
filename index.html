About

I am an MS in Accounting and Fundamental Analysis student at Columbia Business School, graduating in December 2027, focused on private equity, growth equity, credit, and fundamental research. Before Columbia, I was a Private Equity Summer Analyst at 360 ONE Asset Management in Mumbai, working on growth-stage and late-stage investments.

My training is in mathematics. I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at Pomona College, published three peer-reviewed papers in algebra, and began a PhD in Operations Research and Financial Engineering at Princeton on a full fellowship before leaving to build a career in investing. I passed CFA Level I in August 2026.
